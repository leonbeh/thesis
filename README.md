# Deep Learning Approaches for Modelling Economic Time Series and Financial Returns – A Comparative Study with Econometric Time Series Models

## Abstract
This thesis conducts a comparative study of econometric time series models (ARIMA, ARCH/GARCH-type models) and deep learning architectures (FFNN, RNN, LSTM) in forecasting one-step ahead returns and volatility using historical S&P 500 data. It outlines the intricacies of financial time series analysis and forecasting tasks, providing insights into the motivation behind econometric models. By outlining not only their strengths but their weaknesses, this thesis motivates exploring the application of deep learning approaches for modelling economic time series. This comparative study aims to strengthen its robustness by using a 12-fold cross validation approach and exploring alternative volatility proxies. While eleven models are compared across two forecasting tasks, the results suggest that increased complexity does not necessarily correlate with increased accuracy.
